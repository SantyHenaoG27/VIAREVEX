# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two profiles, roughly equal weight:

1. **Professional Latino, 30–45** — travels for pleasure 1–2 times a year. Has money and wants a good experience, but doesn't have the time or knowledge to plan it alone. Wants someone they trust to handle it.
2. **Couples or families celebrating something** — honeymoon, anniversary, milestone birthday, family reunion. The occasion matters; they need everything managed so they can just live it.

Both share the same core context: they're not bargain-hunters. They're willing to pay for real expertise and personal attention. They make decisions via WhatsApp and Instagram, not booking forms.

## Product Purpose

Viarevex is a personal travel advisory. The founder has visited every destination he recommends — not from a catalogue, but from real travel. The service designs custom itineraries, manages every detail (flights, hotels, transfers, experiences), and stays present during the trip. The differentiator is authentic expertise: real experience of real places, not reselling packages.

Primary actions on the site: start a WhatsApp conversation, fill the interactive planner. No e-commerce, no booking engine.

## Brand Personality

Aventurero · Auténtico · Experto

Voice: direct, confident, personal. First person where appropriate. Not corporate. Not influencer-casual either — more like a well-traveled friend who happens to know exactly what they're doing. Bilingual (ES/EN toggle).

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel confident that someone who really knows the world will take care of them.

## Anti-references

- **Booking.com / Expedia / Despegar** — price-first, chaotic, transactional. Viarevex is the opposite of a marketplace.
- **Generic tour operator agencies** — package brochures, stock photos, cookie-cutter itineraries. The antithesis of the personal service Viarevex offers.
- **Ostentatious luxury (excess of gold)** — overloaded, inauthentic, trying too hard. Gold is used here as an accent, not wallpaper. Sophistication without pretension.
- **Influencer travel content** — viral, shallow, no curation or depth.

## Design Principles

1. **Experience over transaction.** The site is not a booking engine. Every section should feel like a conversation, not a catalogue.
2. **Show, don't sell.** The founder's real photos of real destinations carry more weight than any headline. Let imagery do the convincing.
3. **Expertise earns trust.** The personal knowledge behind each recommendation is the product. Surface the "why" — because the founder was there.
4. **Human before process.** The service starts with the person, not the destination. Layout and copy should reflect this order.
5. **Sophisticated restraint.** Navy + gold palette with intention. One accent, not a rainbow. Elegance through what's left out, not what's added.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text)
- Bilingual ES/EN — both languages are first-class
- Reduced motion support already in place (`@media prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Primary interaction channel is WhatsApp — mobile-first matters
